# CotatQ Claims Policy

## Safe claim if v1.2 survives

A suitable claim is:

> On the locked CotatQ v1.2 single-amplitude tensor-network benchmark, the frozen
> CotatQ implementation showed a reproducible performance advantage against the
> tested numerically-valid competitors on the reported hardware/software stack.

Always report:

- exact benchmark version;
- machine/environment;
- competitor versions;
- win rate;
- geometric mean;
- confidence interval;
- accuracy tolerance;
- timeouts/skips;
- whether the run was performed by an independent person/environment.

## Claims not supported

Do not say:

- "CotatQ turns a PC into a quantum computer."
- "CotatQ has quantum advantage."
- "CotatQ is faster than all quantum simulators."
- "CotatQ is a new scientific algorithm" unless novelty is separately established.
- "Peer reviewed" unless it actually is.

## Why

MPS, tensor-network contraction, greedy/random-greedy path search, hyper-optimization,
and incremental graph techniques all have substantial prior art.

A strong implementation result can still be useful and publishable as software,
but performance evidence and algorithmic novelty are different claims.
